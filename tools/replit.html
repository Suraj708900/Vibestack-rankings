Alternatives

  • Lovable: Polished full-stack apps from chat, with Supabase built in. Best pick for non-coders shipping an MVP.
  • Bolt: Fast in-browser builder with a real dev environment. Great for quick prototypes you can export.
  • v0: Best-in-class React/Next.js UI generation that deploys to Vercel. Strongest for front-ends.
  • Lovable: The most beginner-friendly way to get a real app with a backend live.
  • Cursor: The step up when you outgrow builders and want to own the code.
