Hostinger review for builders: plans, pricing, pros & cons

Checked 2026-10-16 on hostinger.com. Prices are the promotional 48-month rates shown in USD excluding VAT; they vary by region, currency and promotion. Always confirm on the official pricing page.

Hostinger is a shared, cloud and VPS web host. Its web hosting page states it is trusted by 5M+ users, includes a free domain, email and SSL, offers a 30-day money-back guarantee and 24/7 support, and supports building with AI, WordPress, drag-and-drop or code (including Node.js on listed plans).

Note the gap between intro and renewal pricing: the low monthly rate requires prepaying a long term, and renewal is roughly 3-4x higher.

Pros

  • Very low entry price on long terms.
  • Free domain for the first year, SSL and email included.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • WordPress plus Node.js support on listed plans; AI website builder credits included.

Cons

  • Headline price needs a 48-month prepay; renewals are much higher.
  • Free domain and mailboxes are for the first year only.
  • Shared hosting is not built for heavy serverless/edge apps: for Next.js or AI apps with server functions, platforms like Vercel, Netlify or Render are usually a better fit.

Who it is for

Bloggers, small businesses, WordPress sites, portfolios and simple stores that want an all-in-one host with domain and email on a tight budget. Less suited to teams deploying git-based full-stack apps who want preview deployments per branch.
