Once your side project has paying users, support email arrives. Most of it repeats: password resets, refund policy, "does it work with X". An AI support agent trained on your docs can answer those in seconds and pass the hard ones to you. These five tools cover chat widgets, ticket resolution and phone support. All are in our [chatbots directory](/directory/chatbots.html) or [Voice rankings](/category/voice.html).

## The top 5 AI customer support agents

### 1. Botpress: best for agents that take actions

[Botpress](/t/botpress.html) is an AI agent platform for customer support. It can look up an order or update a record, not just answer from docs, and its site says it charges no per-seat cost. Paid plans start at $40/mo per its pricing page.

**Verdict:** the most capable builder here once you need workflows and integrations. More to learn than a drop-in widget.

### 2. Chatbase: best for a quick, polished website agent

[Chatbase](/t/chatbase.html) trains an agent on your content and deploys it across channels. Paid plans start at $32/month.

**Verdict:** a fast path to a good-looking chat agent on your site. You trade some flexibility for that speed.

### 3. SiteSpeakAI: best budget option with human handoff

[SiteSpeakAI](/t/sitespeakai.html) trains a chatbot on your site, docs, PDFs and videos, and supports handing a conversation to a human. It offers white-label bots for agencies. Free plan available; paid from $24/month.

**Verdict:** good value for small sites and freelancers who build bots for clients. Fewer advanced automations than Botpress.

### 4. Duckie: best for resolving tickets end to end

[Duckie](/t/duckie-ai.html) builds AI support agents meant to resolve tickets fully, aimed at teams with an existing help desk.

**Verdict:** worth a look if you already handle ticket volume and want automation inside that queue. Overkill for a project with ten emails a week.

### 5. Vapi: best for phone support

[Vapi](/tools/vapi.html) is a developer platform for voice agents, including phone agents that answer calls, handle turn-taking and connect to your systems.

**Verdict:** the right tool if your customers call rather than chat. It is developer-focused, so expect to write some code.

## Before you launch

Write a clear refund and escalation policy first, because the agent will repeat whatever you give it. Always offer a route to a human. Tell users they are talking to an AI. For the build side, see [how to build an AI agent](/blog/how-to-build-an-ai-agent.html) and [the best AI voice tools](/blog/best-ai-voice-tools.html). Developers who want to roll their own can start from our [agent frameworks](/category/agent-frameworks.html).

## FAQ

### What is the best AI chatbot for customer support?
Botpress for agents that take actions; Chatbase or SiteSpeakAI for a quick website agent trained on your docs.

### Can an AI agent answer support phone calls?
Yes. Vapi lets developers build phone agents that answer and respond in real time.

### Should an AI support agent hand off to humans?
Yes. Give it a clear escalation path for refunds, angry customers and anything it cannot answer from your docs.
