AI music tools split into two camps. One writes full songs with vocals from a text prompt. The other produces background tracks you can drop under a video or a game level without worrying about a copyright strike. Mixing them up is the most common mistake we see. Here are five from our [audio and voice directory](/directory/audio-voice.html), ranked by use case.

## The top 5 AI music generators

### 1. Suno: best for full songs with vocals

[Suno](/t/suno.html) turns a prompt or lyrics into a complete song, vocals included. You can make a theme song for your channel or a joke track about a boss fight in a couple of minutes. Its pricing page lists a free plan and paid plans from $8/month.

**Verdict:** the most fun and the most popular song generator right now. Read the license terms before using tracks commercially; rights differ between free and paid plans.

### 2. Udio: best alternative for song generation

[Udio](/t/udio.html) also generates songs from prompts and has its own community for sharing tracks.

**Verdict:** worth trying side by side with Suno, because each handles genres differently. Check the current terms before you publish anything.

### 3. AIVA: best for game and film soundtracks

[AIVA](/t/aiva.html) is built around composition: orchestral, ambient and cinematic pieces you can steer by style. For an indie game that needs a calm exploration loop and a tense combat theme, it is a natural fit. Paid plans start at €11/month per its pricing page.

**Verdict:** the most "composer-like" tool here. Not built for pop songs with vocals.

### 4. Mubert: best for royalty-free background tracks

[Mubert](/t/mubert.html) generates music from text prompts for videos and projects, with a focus on royalty-free use. Paid plans start at $14/month.

**Verdict:** practical for YouTubers and streamers who need endless background music. Tracks can sound generic, which is fine under a voiceover and less fine as a feature.

### 5. Beatoven.ai: best for scoring a specific video

[Beatoven.ai](/t/beatoven-ai.html) creates royalty-free background music and is aimed at creators scoring a particular piece of content.

**Verdict:** a good pick when you want music shaped to one video's mood. Smaller feature set than the others.

## Which one should you use?

Songs with vocals: Suno or Udio. Game soundtracks: AIVA. Background music for streams and videos: Mubert or Beatoven.ai. ## FAQ

### Can I use AI-generated music on YouTube?
Often yes, but it depends on each tool's license and your plan. Check the terms, especially for monetized videos.

### Which AI makes songs with vocals?
Suno and Udio both generate full songs with vocals from a prompt.

### What is the best AI music tool for games?
AIVA, because it focuses on composed, style-driven pieces like soundtracks and loops.
