AIUseFlow FAQ

What is AIUseFlow?

AIUseFlow is an independent directory of AI tools for builders. It ranks coding agents, app builders, databases, hosting, AI APIs and agent frameworks by use case, with short verdicts, pricing notes and links to each tool's official site.

How are tools ranked on AIUseFlow?

Tools are grouped by use case and ranked editorially by how well they fit that job, with free tier and pricing noted on each card. Rankings are never sold, and sponsored listings are always labeled.

Does AIUseFlow use affiliate links?

Yes. Some outbound links are affiliate links, so AIUseFlow may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These links are marked rel="sponsored" and do not change the rankings.

Do I need an account to try the AI playground?

No. The playground on the homepage runs through AIUseFlow's own server, so you can ask an open model a coding or product question without an account or an API key.

Who runs AIUseFlow?

AIUseFlow is owned and run by Suraj Kumar from Patna City, Bihar, India. You can reach the team at contact@aiuseflow.com.
