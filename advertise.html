Questions advertisers ask

Can I pay to rank higher?

No. Rankings are never sold. A sponsored slot is shown separately from the rankings and is labeled.

Is a free listing really free?

Yes. Submit your tool; it is reviewed and listed at no cost if it fits the directory.

Can you share traffic numbers?

Yes, on request. Tell us which pages or categories you are interested in and we will share what we have.

Is Starter or Advanced a subscription?

No. Both are a single payment. Your Featured label and sponsored slot stay while the listing stays approved. Single placements run for their stated period: one week, one year or three months.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or AI assistant mentions?

No. We deliver the article and the technical setup. Rankings and AI citations depend on your product and market, so they are never promised.

How long does it take to go live?

Once dates and payment are confirmed, the placement starts on the agreed date.

Does Advanced buy a positive review?

No. Advanced includes a written article about your tool, clearly labeled Sponsored. We describe what the tool does honestly, and it never changes the tool's rank or score.
